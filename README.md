A phoenix painted in watercolor and ink flies straight out of its own framed painting, right at you. Gold, orange and red washes with dark ink lines and drips make the bird, wings spread wider than the painting itself. The wings have torn clean through the frame's left and right sides — those rails are gone entirely, so behind the wings there is only the dark wall, with broken stubs and splinters at the corners where the rails used to be. The top and bottom rails are still whole, and the tail hangs back inside the painting. Two tiny students crouch below with their hands over their heads, against a plain dark wall. No words anywhere in the picture; the phoenix is the one thing you see, and even at thumbnail size it is unmistakably coming at you. Made for make.fun's [ai-excited-01](https://make.fun/bounty/ai-excited-01) bounty ($250, top 20 paid).

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` checks `entry.png` three ways — JPEG opening bytes, byte count, SHA-256 — and then copies the exact shipped picture to `/output/entry.png`.
