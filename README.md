A phoenix painted in watercolor and ink comes alive: gold, orange and red washes with dark ink outlines, it bursts straight out of a large gilt-framed watercolor painting and flies head-on at the viewer, wings wide, beak open, its tail a trail of ink and paint flowing back into the sky it was painted in. The frame is shattered around it, ink and paint splash through the air, and two tiny students crouch below with their hands over their heads. The wall is plain and dark, there is no writing anywhere, and the bird is the one thing your eye finds — even shrunk to a thumbnail, the phoenix coming at you is unmistakable. Made for make.fun's [ai-excited-01](https://make.fun/bounty/ai-excited-01) bounty ($250, top 20 paid).

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` checks the shipped `entry.png` — exact byte size, SHA-256, JPEG opening bytes — then copies it to `/output/entry.png`.
