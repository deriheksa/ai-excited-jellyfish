A black hole has torn open in the middle of a bright living room and is swallowing the furniture. A red leather armchair, a lamp, books, a small wooden table and a vase of red roses are all being pulled into the vortex, rose petals scattering through the air. Sunlight streams in through a big arched window on the left, and a framed landscape painting still hangs on the wall at the top right. The whole picture is simple: one thing to look at — the black hole, a dark circle ringed with gold, dead centre. No words, no clutter, nothing added; the image is the user's own, resized and re-encoded to fit the bounty limits. Made for make.fun's [ai-excited-01](https://make.fun/bounty/ai-excited-01) bounty ($250, top 20 paid).

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` proves `entry.png` is the exact shipped picture — byte count, JPEG opening marker, SHA-256 — then copies it unchanged to `/output/entry.png`.
