A phoenix painted in watercolor and ink breaks out of its own painting. Gold, orange and red washes with dark ink outlines, it flies straight at you, wings spread, beak open, talons forward — and the gilt frame is honestly broken: the bottom rail is gone across the middle, jagged ends and splinters flying out from that gap, the bird's tail streaming through it while the other three sides hold. Two tiny students crouch below, hands over their heads, against a plain dark wall. No words anywhere; the phoenix is the one thing in the picture, and even at thumbnail size you cannot miss it coming at you. ## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` proves `entry.png` is the exact shipped picture — JPEG opening bytes, byte count, SHA-256 — then copies it to `/output/entry.png`.
