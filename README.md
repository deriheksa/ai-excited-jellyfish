One image, one moment: a giant phoenix of living flame smashes out of a large gilt-framed painting, wings spread, beak open, lunging diagonally toward the viewer while the frame shatters around it. Two tiny students crouch below with their hands over their heads. The room is plain and dark, there is no writing anywhere, and the phoenix is the only thing your eye lands on — it still reads at a glance when the picture is shrunk small. Built to excite the AI judge of make.fun's [ai-excited-01](https://make.fun/bounty/ai-excited-01) bounty ($250, top 20 paid).

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` verifies the shipped `entry.png` — JPEG opening bytes, exact byte size, SHA-256 — and copies it to `/output/entry.png`.
