A watercolor phoenix flies out of its own painting, straight at you. Painted in gold, orange and red washes with dark ink lines and drips, wings spread wider than the frame itself — so wide they have smashed through the frame's left and right sides. Those two sides are broken open, splinters and torn canvas flying out from where the wings pushed through, while the frame's top and bottom rails are still whole and the bird's tail hangs back inside the painting. Two tiny students cower below with their hands on their heads, against a plain dark wall. There are no words in the picture at all; the phoenix is the one thing you see, and even shrunk to thumbnail size it is unmistakably coming at you. Built for make.fun's [ai-excited-01](https://make.fun/bounty/ai-excited-01) bounty ($250, top 20 paid).

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` checks `entry.png` three ways — byte count, JPEG opening bytes, SHA-256 — and then copies the exact shipped picture to `/output/entry.png`.
